How to get it right every time.

# Preparation

## Step 1

Pour a third of the {{ 150g of white sugar }} with {{ 1 tbsp of water }} into a heavy-bottomed saucepan and heat over *medium heat*.

## Step 2

As soon as it melts and turns liquid at the edges, add the second third. Then add the last third once the previous one has melted.

## Step 3

*Never stir*. Gently tilt the pan if needed to even it out.

## Step 4

Remove from the heat as soon as the caramel is golden (or gives off a light smoke).
