# Preparation

## Step 1

Mix {{ 30g of rolled oats }}, {{ 60g of pumpkin seeds }}, {{ 30g of sunflower seeds }}, {{ 130g of buckwheat flour }} and {{ 1/2 tsp of salt }}.

## Step 2

Stir {{ 130ml of water }} and {{ 20g of olive oil }} into the dry mix.

## Step 3 (Optional)

Add {{ 60g of grated cheese }} or {{ spices of your choice }}.

## Step 4

Lay down a first {{ sheet of baking paper }}. Spread the mixture on it, place a second sheet on top, and flatten with a rolling pin.

## Step 5

Score lines or triangles. Carefully remove the top sheet.

## Step 6

Bake at 150ºC for about 1 hour.
