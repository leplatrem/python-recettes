A budget-friendly classic.

# Preparation

## Step 1

Mix {{ 3 mugs of rolled oats }}, {{ 4 tbsp of pure cocoa }}, {{ 3 tbsp of grated coconut }}, {{ 1 tbsp of sesame seeds }}, {{ 2 tbsp of flaked almonds }}.

## Step 2

Stir in {{ 1/2 mug of coconut oil }}, {{ 1/4 mug of rice syrup }}, {{ 1/4 mug of agave syrup }}, {{ 1 tbsp of vanilla extract }}.

## Step 3

Spread on {{ baking paper }} and bake at 180ºC for 10min.

## Step 4

Stir, and bake for another 6min.

## Step 5

Let cool and break into pieces.
