# Preparation

## Step 1

Squeeze {{ 0.5L of lemon juice }}.

## Step 2

Peel {{ 300g of fresh ginger }} and grind it in a food processor.

## Step 3

Simmer the mixture over low heat with {{ 0.5L of water }} and {{ 100g of sugar }}.

## Step 4

Blend the result.
