Buckwheat Crêpe Batter

preparation
3min
healthygluten-free

Ingredients

8×
  • 300 g of buckwheat flour
  • 10 g of salt
  • 1 egg
  • 750 ml of water

For Breton savoury crêpes (galettes).

Preparation

Mix 300 g of buckwheat flour, 10 g of salt, 1 egg, then gradually add 750 ml of water, keeping the batter smooth.
