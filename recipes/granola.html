Crunchy Granola

preparation
5min
cooking
16min
breakfasthealthygluten-freebudget-friendly

Ingredients

×
  • 3 mugs of rolled oats
  • 4 tbsp of pure cocoa
  • 3 tbsp of grated coconut
  • 1 tbsp of sesame seeds
  • 2 tbsp of flaked almonds
  • 0.5 mug of coconut oil
  • 0.25 mug of rice syrup
  • 0.25 mug of agave syrup
  • 1 tbsp of vanilla extract
  • baking paper

A budget-friendly classic.

Preparation

Step 1

Mix 3 mugs of rolled oats, 4 tbsp of pure cocoa, 3 tbsp of grated coconut, 1 tbsp of sesame seeds, 2 tbsp of flaked almonds.

Step 2

Stir in 0.5 mug of coconut oil, 0.25 mug of rice syrup, 0.25 mug of agave syrup, 1 tbsp of vanilla extract.

Step 3

Spread on baking paper and bake at 180ºC for 10min.

Step 4

Stir, and bake for another 6min.

Step 5

Let cool and break into pieces.
