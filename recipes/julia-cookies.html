Ingredients

9×
  • 115 g of butter
  • 160 g of flour
  • a pinch of salt
  • 0.5 tsp of baking soda
  • 70 g of white sugar
  • 80 g of brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp of vanilla extract
  • chocolate chips

Julia’s version.

Preparation

Step 1

Preheat the oven to 200ºC.

Step 2

Brown 115 g of butter in a pan (6-7min).

Step 3

In a first bowl, mix 160 g of flour, a pinch of salt, and 0.5 tsp of baking soda.

Step 4

Wait for the butter to cool down.

In a second bowl, mix 70 g of white sugar with 80 g of brown sugar, and stir in the butter.

Add 1 egg and 1 tsp of vanilla extract, and mix well.

Step 5

Combine both bowls without overmixing, to keep some texture, then add the chocolate chips.

Step 6

Bake for 8 to 10min.
