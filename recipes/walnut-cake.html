Walnut Cake

preparation
15min
cooking
30min
dessert

Ingredients

×
  • 250 g of walnuts
  • 6 egg yolks
  • 200 g of sugar
  • 50 g of breadcrumbs
  • 6 egg whites

From Mamie Lucette.

Preparation

Preheat the oven to 180ºC.

Step 1

Blend 250 g of walnuts.

Step 2

Beat the 6 egg yolks with 200 g of sugar. Stir in the walnuts and 50 g of breadcrumbs.

Step 3

Gently fold in 6 egg whites beaten to stiff peaks.

Step 4

Bake for 30min.
